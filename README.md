# Eventions IPO: GMP Today, Price Band, Dates and Allotment Status

The [Eventions IPO](https://www.finowings.com/IPO/eventions-ipo) is a ₹38.12 crore SME issue that opens on 30 September 2026 and closes on 5 October 2026. The shares are set to list on NSE SME. Below you will find the price, GMP, key dates and allotment details to review before you bid.

## About Eventions

Eventions Limited was established in 2020 and works in corporate event management. It handles meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions, often called MICE, for business clients. The company follows an asset-light B2B model and has handled assignments in India as well as in Europe, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific.

## Eventions IPO Price and Price Band

The Eventions IPO price band is ₹112 to ₹118 per share, with a face value of ₹10. The final issue price will be set within this range once book building ends. The issue is fully fresh, with 32,30,400 shares and no offer for sale, so all the money raised goes to the company.

## Lot Size and Minimum Investment

One lot has 1,200 shares, which costs ₹1,41,600 at the upper band. Retail investors must apply for at least 2 lots, or 2,400 shares, so the minimum retail investment is ₹2,83,200. HNI applications start at 3 lots, which is ₹4,24,800 at the upper band. Some portals list only the single lot amount, so confirm the figure with your broker.

## Eventions IPO Date, Open Date and Closing Date

The Eventions IPO open date is 30 September 2026. The last date to apply, also the closing date, is 5 October 2026. Submit your bid early on the final day, because UPI mandate approvals can take time.

Allotment is expected on 6 October 2026. To check your allotment status, go to the registrar's website or the NSE portal and enter your application number or PAN. Refunds and demat credit should follow on 7 October 2026, and the listing is scheduled for 8 October 2026.

## Should You Apply?

SME issues carry more risk than mainboard IPOs because of lower liquidity and larger price swings. Read the red herring prospectus, study revenue and profit trends, and check how the fresh funds will be used. Event businesses can depend on a few large clients, so look at client concentration too. Invest only what you can afford to lock in.
